Fact vs Fiction

Mental Health: Get the Facts

Learn the truth behind common mental health myths and help break stigma.

Fiction: Mental health problems only affect adults.
Fact: Mental health challenges can affect people at any age, including children and teens. Early support can make a lifelong difference.
Fiction: Feeling sad or anxious means something is wrong with you.
Fact: Everyone experiences sadness and anxiety at times. These emotions are part of being human and don’t mean you’re broken.
Fact: Mental health is just as important as physical health.
Explanation: Mental health influences how we think, feel, and act—just like physical health affects how our bodies function.
Fiction: You can always tell when someone is struggling.
Fact: Many people hide what they’re going through, even from close friends and family. You never truly know what someone is facing.
Fact: Talking about mental health can help.
Explanation: Opening up to someone can reduce feelings of isolation and help people feel supported and understood.
Fiction: Strong people don’t struggle with mental health.
Fact: Mental health challenges affect people regardless of strength, success, or confidence. Asking for help is a form of strength.
Fiction: Mental health challenges are a sign of weakness.
Fact: Many people grow stronger after working through mental health challenges and gain better coping skills and self-awareness.
