Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about using YouMatter and the community forums.

How do I post in the forums?
Open Forums, choose a topic, and use the reply form. You can post with a display name or anonymously.
Is my information private?
We only store what you submit to a post or reply. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information. See What We Do for more.
Where can I get immediate help?
Visit Get Help for crisis resources and support options.
