Is the equipment in the studio for hire?
We currently don’t rent equipment externally. For studio use or session bookings, please contact us via the Email Us button.
Do you run classes in glassblowing, coldworking or flameworking?
Yes, we run beginner-friendly workshops. See Services → Courses for dates and topics.
I need some grinding and polishing done — do you do this?
We can help on a case-by-case basis. Share details of your piece and timeline via Email Us.
Do you repair glass objects?
Minor repairs may be possible depending on the damage. Send a photo and description to discuss options.
