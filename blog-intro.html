    Intro Blog

    Dec 2025 · Introduction

    Welcome to our website about mental health awareness made by Rishit Lalchandani and Dhairya Mehta. In this blog, we will talk about the features we have on our website, like the home screen, the disorders section, the blogs section, and the about us section.

    Anyway, to help you get started, let's quickly walk through each one of these sections I just mentioned above.

    Firstly, the home screen is the main page you see when you first open the website, and is designed for easy navigation so users can easily find the important information they are looking for. Now, let's dive into some of the features on the home screen:

    • Our latest blog posts (like this one!) on various topics like mental health awareness and real-life people who have gone through this experience
    • Quick links to key resources and support hotlines.
    • Important statistics to help showcase the importance of mental health awareness

    Secondly, the disorders section, a tab you can find after clicking the button in the top right corner named "disorders." This section introduces various types of disorders and their effects. Some disabilities in this section include:

    • Anxiety Disorders
    • OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder)
    • Depression
    • ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder)
    • PTSD (Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder)
    • Bipolar Disorder
    • Eating Disorders
    • Suicide/Crisis Help (MHA)

    Thirdly, we have the Blogs Section, this is where we share in-depth articles, personal stories, mental health awareness, and how-to about such disorders effectively.

    Fourthly, the About Us Section... Here you can learn more about us, Rishit Lalchandani and Dhairya Mehta, and our mission statement, which defines our goals and what we are trying to achieve with this website.

    Finally, we have the Forums section, where you can interact with others who might be going through a similar situation or disorder as you. This forum helps connect like-minded individuals together to just talk about life and other issues they might have on a day-to-day basis.
